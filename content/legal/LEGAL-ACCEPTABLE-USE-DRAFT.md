## 1. The short version
This is a working app for a working trade. Book cuts, do good work, show it, get paid.

Three lines you don't cross:
1. **Don't make the chair unsafe** — for a client, a barber, or a shop.
2. **Don't fake the truth** — of a booking, a review, a license, a payment, or who you are.
3. **Don't take the money outside the room** — no scams, no laundering, no lying about what a service costs.

Everything below is detail on those three.

## 2. Safety and respect
Not allowed anywhere on BVRB3R — profiles, Culture, messages, reviews, kiosk entries, shop names:

- Threats, intimidation, or incitement of violence
- Harassment or repeated unwanted contact after someone disengages
- Hate speech or slurs targeting race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Sexual content, sexual solicitation, or sexualizing anyone
- Content involving minors that a parent wouldn't consent to being public
- Doxxing — posting someone's address, phone, workplace, or documents without consent
- Impersonating another barber, shop, or person

**In the shop:** a barber may refuse service; a client may leave. Neither is a violation. Retaliating with fake reviews, mass reports, or harassment **is**.

## 3. Truth of the trade
- **License claims must be real.** Uploading someone else's license, an expired one, or an altered document is fraud and ends the account.
- **Shop claims must be real.** Don't list a shop you don't operate. Don't claim a verified badge you didn't earn.
- **Portfolio work must be yours.** Posting another barber's cut as your own is theft of their work. Credit or don't post it.
- **Reviews must come from real visits.** No paying for reviews, trading reviews, reviewing yourself, or review-bombing a competitor.
- **Availability must be honest.** Don't hold slots you won't work or run a chair as bait.
- **Identity must be yours.** One person, one account. Shops and barbers are separate lanes of the same account, not disguises.

## 4. Money
- No charging outside the app to dodge fees you agreed to, then using the app's booking, receipts, or protections anyway
- No fake or duplicate transactions, no testing cards, no laundering
- No chargeback abuse — disputing a service you received and kept
- No misrepresenting a price, a fee, a deposit, or a refund rule
- No using gifted cuts, gift cards, or loyalty rewards as a cash-out scheme
- **Booth rent is rent.** Structuring payments to disguise a commission arrangement violates this policy and the rent agreement

Cash is fine. Cash that never gets recorded, when the app says it was, is not.

## 5. Kiosks and shared devices
A kiosk sits in public, and someone else touches it next.

- Don't leave a session open on purpose or defeat the between-client wipe
- Don't book a walk-in under a name that isn't theirs
- Don't use a kiosk to collect contact information for anything but the booking
- Don't disable the exit PIN or hand it to someone who shouldn't have it
- Owners: don't configure a kiosk to hide a wait, misstate a price, or route a client to a barber who didn't agree to take them

## 6. Culture
Culture is the trade's feed. Post your work, your shop, your day.

Not allowed: stolen work · unrelated advertising or spam · off-platform booking links designed to route around the app · engagement-farming (follow/unfollow, comment spam) · bulk-identical posts across accounts.

We may remove a post, limit reach, or lock posting for repeat violations. We tell you which rule and give you a way to respond.

## 7. Security and the platform
No scraping, bulk-downloading, or hammering our APIs · no probing, penetration testing, or bypassing rate limits without written permission · no reverse-engineering to extract another user's data · no uploading malware · no automating account creation.

Found a real vulnerability? Tell us at security@bvrb3r.app before telling anyone else. We'll work with you.

## 8. Reporting and what happens next
**Report:** ⋯ → Report on any profile, post, message, review, or shop. Tell us what happened; more detail resolves it faster.

**What we do:** review it, look at the record, and act. Depending on severity: nothing (not a violation) · a warning · content removed · a feature limited · an account suspended · an account terminated · law enforcement, where required.

**Money is never a weapon.** A suspension does not vaporize what someone is owed. Rent ledgers settle, payouts release per the payout schedule, and disputed lines stay disputable — the same settle-first rule as everywhere else in the app.

**Immediate action** is reserved for credible threats of harm, sexual content involving minors, verified identity fraud, and active financial fraud.

## 9. Appeals
If we act on your account, we tell you what rule and how to appeal. Appeals are reviewed by someone other than whoever made the original call. We aim to answer within [X] business days. *Counsel to set.*

## 10. Changes
We'll post material changes in the app before they take effect. If a change would make something you're already doing a violation, we'll tell you and give you time to adjust.
