## 1. The short version
Three rules govern every refund in this app:

1. **You see the rule before you commit.** The cancellation window, the fee, the deposit — all shown before you confirm, and again on your receipt.
2. **The barber's policy governs the haircut.** BVRB3R doesn't set it and doesn't take a cut of it.
3. **A policy change never touches a booking you already made.** Your appointment keeps the rule that was live when you booked it.

## 2. Who refunds what
| Money | Whose policy | Who refunds |
|---|---|---|
| Service payment | The barber's, disclosed at booking | The barber, via Stripe |
| Tip | Always refundable on request before the cut | The barber |
| No-show / late-cancel fee | The barber's, disclosed at booking | The barber |
| Deposit | The barber's, disclosed at booking | The barber |
| Booth rent | The booth rent agreement between barber and shop | The shop owner |
| BVRB3R subscription | This policy, §6 | BVRB3R |
| Gifted cut | Not refundable in cash — returns to the pool | — |
| Gift card | Per §7 | BVRB3R |

BVRB3R never keeps a percentage of a service, so a service refund is a return of the barber's money, not ours.

## 3. Client cancellations
**Inside the free window** — full refund, automatically, no conversation needed. We show you the amount before you confirm the cancellation.

**Outside the free window** — the barber's late-cancel fee applies. We name the fee, show what you'll be refunded, and require you to confirm before anything is charged.

**No-show** — the barber's no-show fee applies. It charges to your card on file and goes to the barber.

**No card on file** — an unpaid fee becomes a balance. Your account is limited until it's settled, with every line itemized and disputable (see Terms §5).

## 4. When the barber or shop cancels
**You are never charged.** Full refund of anything already paid, including deposits, and we tell you why it was cancelled.

If a barber cancels within 24 hours of your appointment, we also offer the nearest open times at that shop and, where available, other barbers who can take you.

**Shop closure, emergency, or kiosk outage** — same: full refund, no fee, and the cancellation reason recorded.

## 5. When something went wrong with the cut
We are not the judge of whether a haircut was good. That's between you and your barber, and most of it resolves in one conversation at the chair.

**Talk to your barber first.** Many will fix it, discount it, or comp the next one.

**If money is genuinely wrong** — charged twice, charged the wrong amount, charged for a service you didn't get, charged after a valid cancellation — open a dispute in the app. We look at the record: what was booked, what the policy said, what was charged, what the ledger shows. We resolve on the facts, usually within [X] business days. *Counsel to set the committed window.*

**What we will not do:** override a barber's disclosed policy because you changed your mind, or refund a service you received because you didn't like the result.

## 6. Subscriptions
**Cancel any time.** You keep access through the period you already paid for. We don't refund a period you used.

**Upgrades** apply immediately and are prorated against your current period.

**Downgrades** apply at the end of the paid period, so you never lose access you paid for.

**Failed payment** — we retry on a published schedule (1 day, 3 days, 7 days) and tell you each time. After the grace period your plan drops to the free floor; nothing is deleted, and your data waits for you.

**Duplicate or wrong charge on a subscription** — we refund it in full, promptly. That's our error, not a policy question.

**Annual plans** — *counsel to advise on prorated cancellation, and on any state-specific auto-renewal disclosure and cancellation-ease requirements (e.g. California ARL).*

## 7. Gift cards, gifted cuts, and loyalty
**Gift cards** — not refundable for cash except where law requires it. They don't expire and don't carry fees. *Counsel to confirm against state gift-card statutes.*

**Gifted cuts** — a community gift, not stored value. If a client declines one, it returns to the barber's pool. It is never converted to cash for anyone.

**Loyalty rewards** — earned, not purchased, so they carry no cash value and aren't refundable.

## 8. Chargebacks
If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of us, we may hold related funds while it resolves and we'll cooperate with your bank's process. We'd rather you came to us first — it's faster, and we can see the whole record.

If a chargeback reverses money already paid out to a barber, that becomes a balance on the barber's account, itemized and disputable — never a silent deduction.

## 9. How refunds arrive
Card refunds go back to the original card via Stripe, typically in 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Cash payments are settled in cash at the shop. We show the refund reference on your receipt either way.
