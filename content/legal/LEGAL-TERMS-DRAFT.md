## 1. The short version
BVRB3R connects people who want haircuts with barbers who cut hair, and gives barbers and shop owners the tools to run their businesses.

Three things are true throughout these terms, and we say them here so nothing surprises you later:

1. **We are not your barber and not their boss.** Barbers are independent professionals. They set their own prices, hours, and services. Shop owners rent chairs. BVRB3R is software, not a salon.
2. **We never take a cut of a haircut.** Barbers keep what they charge and 100% of tips. Shop owners earn booth rent from barbers. BVRB3R earns subscription fees. There is no commission anywhere in this app.
3. **Money moves through Stripe.** Nothing else. Other calendars can be connected for scheduling, but no other platform moves BVRB3R money.

If any plain-English summary in these terms conflicts with a detailed section, the detailed section controls.

## 2. Who can use BVRB3R
You must be at least 18 to hold an account. A parent or guardian may book for a minor using their own account and is responsible for that appointment.

Barbers and shop owners must hold every license, permit, and registration their state and locality require, and must keep them current. We may ask for proof and may suspend a chair or a shop if it lapses.

## 3. Accounts and roles
| Role | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Books cuts, pays barbers, posts to Culture |
| **Barber** | Independent professional. Own prices, services, hours, clients |
| **Shop owner** | Rents chairs to barbers and runs the floor |
| **Guest** | Books with a verified phone or email before creating an account |

One person may hold more than one role. Roles stay separate: money, client lists, and permissions never cross between them.

You are responsible for your account and everything done through it. Keep your credentials private. Tell us promptly if you lose control of your account.

**Guest booking.** You can book with a verified phone number or email without creating a full account. We hold that booking, receipt, and queue position for you and let you claim it into an account later. A guest booking is a real commitment, with the same cancellation and no-show terms as any other.

## 4. Bookings
When you confirm a booking, you are agreeing to that barber's price, duration, and policies, which we show you before you confirm.

**Your barber's policies govern your appointment**, including lead time, reschedule window, cancellation window, no-show fee, late-cancel fee, and deposits. Those are set by the barber, disclosed before you confirm, and shown again on your receipt. Changing a policy never changes an appointment that was already booked — an existing booking keeps the policy that was live when you made it.

**Holds.** While you are checking out, we hold your slot briefly and show you the countdown. If the hold expires or someone confirms first, we tell you plainly and offer the nearest open times. Nothing is charged for an expired hold.

**Walk-ins and kiosk.** A kiosk booking is a booking. Queue position and wait estimates are our best calculation from live shop data, not a guarantee.

**Cancellation and no-show.** We show the refund rule before you commit and again if you cancel. If a fee applies, we name it, charge it to your card on file, and pass it to the barber. If a barber cancels on you, you are not charged.

## 5. Money
**Clients pay barbers.** Through the app via Stripe, or in cash at the chair.

**Barbers set prices.** BVRB3R does not set, cap, or suggest a barber's price, and does not take a percentage of any service.

**Tips are 100% the barber's.** The client chooses the tip amount. No barber, shop owner, or BVRB3R employee can select a tip on a client's behalf.

**Shop owners earn booth rent only.** A booth rent agreement between a barber and a shop owner is a contract between *them*, and BVRB3R is not a party to it. We provide the ledger and the payment mechanics.

**AutoBooth rent** is an optional way for a barber to pay rent from earnings: the barber authorizes a percentage, it applies only until that period's rent reaches zero, and it stops automatically. It is a payment channel, never a second income stream for the shop, never additive, and never a commission. The barber may revoke authorization for future periods at any time.

**Cash** is recorded as owed and marked settled only when the transfer actually happens.

**Subscriptions.** BVRB3R charges subscription fees by plan and role (see the in-app plan screen for current prices). Plans renew automatically until cancelled. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the end of the paid period. Cancel any time — you keep access through the period you paid for.

**Past due and balances.** If a payment fails, we retry on a published schedule and tell you each time. If you owe BVRB3R money — a failed subscription payment, a reversed charge, an unpaid fee — we may limit your account until it is paid. When we do, we show you every line item, what it was for, and that nothing is deleted. You may dispute any line, and disputing a line pauses collection on that line while we review it.

**Refunds.** Service refunds follow the barber's disclosed policy. Where we are at fault, we make it right. Subscription fees are not refundable for a period you already used, except where law requires otherwise.

**Taxes.** Barbers and shop owners are responsible for their own taxes. We provide records, not tax advice.

## 6. Content and Culture
**You own what you post.** By posting to Culture, you grant BVRB3R a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, display, and distribute that content inside the app and in app promotion — *counsel to confirm scope*. You can delete a post at any time; we remove it going forward and are not required to retrieve copies others already saved or shared.

**Only post what is yours to post.** Don't post someone else's work as your own. Don't post a client's face without their permission.

**We may remove content** that breaks these terms, breaks the law, or endangers someone. We'll tell you why, and you can appeal.

**Reviews** must reflect a real visit. We don't remove a review for being unflattering. We do remove reviews that are fake, paid, threatening, or contain private information.

## 7. Connected calendars
You may connect Square, Booksy, theCut, Apple Calendar, or Google Calendar so your schedule stays in one place. Understand exactly what that does:

- We read appointment times so your BVRB3R calendar reflects your real availability
- We may write busy-blocks back where the provider supports it
- **We never move money through a connected provider.** Payments made on another platform stay on that platform
- **We never show one barber's external financial data to a shop owner or another barber.** Counts and times only
- You can disconnect at any time; we stop syncing going forward

## 8. What you may not do
Don't use BVRB3R to break the law. Don't harass, threaten, or discriminate. Don't take payment off-platform to avoid fees you owe, or misrepresent who is cutting hair. Don't scrape, reverse-engineer, or resell the app. Don't create accounts to evade a suspension. Don't manipulate ratings, referrals, or gifted cuts. Don't attempt to reach data that isn't yours.

**Gifted cuts** are a goodwill feature. Don't use them to launder payments, evade fees, or manufacture activity. The barber is paid their full price on each gifted cut used; the gift covers the service only, and the tip remains the client's to give.

## 9. Verification badges
A verification badge means we checked a specific document at a specific time. It is not a warranty of skill, safety, or outcome, and it can be removed if the underlying credential lapses.

## 10. Disputes about a haircut
We are not the referee of whether a cut was good. Talk to your barber first — most things resolve in one conversation. If money is genuinely wrong, open a dispute in the app and we will look at the record: what was booked, what was charged, what the policy said. If you use your bank's chargeback process, we may hold related funds while it resolves.

## 11. Availability and changes
We work to keep BVRB3R running, but we don't promise it will never be down. Some features are unfinished, plan-restricted, or temporarily closed; when that is the case we say so plainly on the screen instead of hiding it.

We may change these terms. For material changes we will give notice in the app and by your chosen channel before they take effect, and continuing to use BVRB3R after that means you accept them. Prior versions stay available.

## 12. Ending things
You may close your account at any time from Account → Data & Deletion. We keep records we are legally required to keep — completed transactions, tax records, dispute history — and delete the rest on our published schedule.

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, endangers someone, or creates legal risk. Except in urgent cases, we tell you why first and give you a chance to fix it. **We will not strand your money:** pending payouts and settlements complete under normal timelines, and existing booked appointments are honored or refunded.
