## Canonical Domain Truth

- Appointments: `appointments` is the authoritative appointment row; `appointment_status_history` preserves lifecycle history; `appointment_services` stores booking service snapshots; operations providers and booking routes mutate that state.
- Payments: `payments` is the payment ledger; `payment_routing_records` is the reconciliation/routing truth; `payout_executions` is the payout execution ledger; refunds and tips are separate canonical ledgers.
- Verification: `verification_profiles` is the review subject state; `verification_reviews` is the review action history; `barber_verifications` and `shop_verifications` are lane-specific evidence/status records; `platform_admin_audit_logs` preserves admin action auditability.
- Rewards/loyalty: `points_transactions` is the points ledger; `user_points_balances` is derived/current balance truth; `reward_eligibility_snapshots` explains guardrails; `cashout_requests` owns cash-out workflow state.
- Cross-domain events: `platform_events` is the shared structured transition stream for later AI, analytics, debugging, and support investigations.
